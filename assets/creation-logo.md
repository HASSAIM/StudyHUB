# Logo StudyHUB

Créé avec l’outil intégré ImageGen, puis décliné en tailles d’icône avec les outils macOS. Symbole original de livre et de voix, monogramme S/H, vert forêt et ivoire. Direction éditoriale sobre et affirmée. Symbole original, sans copie d’une marque existante.

## Prompt de création

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished app icon symbol for StudyHUB, a personal university learning application that turns recorded speech and teacher documents into structured courses. Create ONE original polished logo mark, centered, large, square canvas, genuine transparent background. Direction: restrained editorial design, confident architectural geometry, premium apothecary-like restraint and independent fashion sensibility, but entirely original. Symbol: a compact custom interlocking S / H monogram whose two folded page-like strokes subtly suggest an open book, with a single simple rising waveform-shaped notch built into the negative space, suggesting spoken knowledge becoming a written course. Extremely legible at 32 pixels. Solid deep forest green #234538 and warm ivory #F3EFE5 only, optional small muted sage #A7B297 detail if necessary. Flat vector-like crisp silhouettes, strong distinctive negative space, harmonious proportions, no thin lines. No text or letters printed around the symbol, no words, no brand name, no slogan, no mockup, no frame, no border, no background rectangle, no 3D, no shadow, no glow, no gradient, no graduation cap, no brain, no lightbulb, no microphone pictogram, no crosses, no familiar existing brand marks. Output a single usable mark only, with generous but moderate transparent padding.
